🔍 Threat Detector

Paste an email, SMS, WhatsApp message or URL. The engine scores it for phishing and manipulation tactics.

🧠 Human Risk Assessment

Choose what you would honestly do. You will get a personal vulnerability score and tips.

📊 Model Performance

A Naive Bayes text classifier is trained live in your browser on a labelled dataset of phishing and legitimate messages, then evaluated with 5-fold cross-validation.

Final threat score = 55% rule-based signal score (10 tactic detectors + URL analysis) + 45% Naive Bayes phishing probability. This hybrid approach catches both known tricks and new wording.

⚙️ How It Works

System pipeline

Input

Message text, email body or link is captured from the user.

Preprocessing

Lower-casing, tokenisation, URL and entity extraction.

Feature Detection

NLP rules detect urgency, fear, authority, reward and credential requests.

Risk Scoring

Weighted model produces a 0–100 threat score and verdict.

Human Profiling

Behavioural quiz maps user habits to a vulnerability level.

Recommendations

Personalised training tips and safe-action guidance.

📘 About the Project

Objective: reduce successful social engineering attacks by combining automated threat detection with human-factor risk measurement.

Attack types covered: phishing, smishing, vishing scripts, pretexting, baiting, CEO fraud, tech-support scams.

Technologies: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, weighted rule-based NLP scoring (can be upgraded to a trained ML classifier such as Naive Bayes or BERT).

Future scope: browser extension, email-gateway integration, organisation-wide dashboards, multilingual detection.
